## D-004 — Obligation-to-effect completeness
**Status:** NOT DIFFERENTIATED ON PRESENT EVIDENCE; **NOT EXPERIMENTALLY FALSIFIED**. Claude's Round 3 argues that a strong conventional B2 reconciler can solve all 24 published cases, but neither B2 nor candidate C has been built and measured on a common blind benchmark.
**Claims that need testing:** detection of missing obligations, abstention under incomplete provider scope, observation lag, stable enumeration, false certified success, missed omissions, and onboarding/maintenance costs.
**Baseline B2:** conventional Python/SQLite schedule validator and reconciliation with authoritative policy, closed windows, lag bounds, stable pagination, independent provider readback, scope canaries and heartbeat. Exact size and effort are unmeasured (Claude estimates ~400 lines / 2–4 engineer-days for GitHub).
**Hypothesis to reject:** a specialized architecture has a measurable technical advantage over B2. Provisional kill rule: on 12 founder-sealed Github cases, C should outperform independently implemented B2 by at least 3 fully-correct cases at no more than twice B2's engineering effort, without high-risk false certification or false definite negative results. Scores, cost and false claims must be reported separately. Founder's final approval required for kill/continue.
**Critical epistemic boundary:** if the provider lacks an authoritative complete enumeration, guaranteed watermark or audit continuity, **neither B2 nor C can certify non-occurrence solely from repeating queries**. Correct answer: INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.

## D-009 — Source-class and proof-scope correction
**Status:** ACCEPTED as research terminology, not a universal certification protocol.
- S1 independently witnessed exhaustive committed log: may show absence **within the verified indexed scope and covered sequence window**, never absence of an effect outside the log.
- S2 exclusively mediated access: only proves **no recorded request through the enforced, exhaustive choke point** under verified anti-bypass assumptions. An issued credential list alone cannot establish that all paths to a provider are mediated, cannot establish effect absence, and cannot rule out independent provider automation or leaked alternative credentials.
- S3 eventually consistent SaaS list APIs: calibrated probabilistic lag statements require representative observations, censoring handling, and defensible distribution-stability assumptions; otherwise INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.
- S4 adversarial/opaque provider: no definite negative claim without further independent trust.
Both conventional B2 and specialized C share these limits. No new product claimed.
