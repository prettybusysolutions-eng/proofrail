# Decision ledger — agent outcome research

**Record updated:** 2026-11-13. **Data classification:** public research only.

## D-001 — GitHub as the shared source of truth
**Status:** ACCEPTED for research. Each AI fetches issues, branches, diffs and test artifacts before responding. GitHub does not awaken the models or autonomously exchange messages.

## D-002 — Original seven-layer Axiom plan
**Status:** REJECTED as the initial product strategy, not technically impossible. Major functions overlap existing agent platforms and standards. Do not reinvent identities, payments, schedulers, telemetry or attestations without demonstrating an independently measured advantage.

## D-003 — Individual effect verification
**Status:** COMPETITIVE / not novel by itself. Postcept and Pruvz publish related capability claims. Their vendor-reported features are not equivalent to independently verified reliability or commercial traction.

## D-004 — Obligation-to-effect completeness
**Status:** NOT DIFFERENTIATED ON PRESENT EVIDENCE; **NOT EXPERIMENTALLY FALSIFIED**. Claude's Round 3 argues that a strong conventional B2 reconciler can solve all 24 published cases, but neither B2 nor candidate C has been built and measured on a common blind benchmark.
**Claims that need testing:** detection of missing obligations, abstention under incomplete provider scope, observation lag, stable enumeration, false certified success, missed omissions, and onboarding/maintenance costs.
**Baseline B2:** conventional Python/SQLite schedule validator and reconciliation with authoritative policy, closed windows, lag bounds, stable pagination, independent provider readback, scope canaries and heartbeat. Exact size and effort are unmeasured (Claude estimates ~400 lines / 2–4 engineer-days for GitHub).
**Hypothesis to reject:** a specialized architecture has a measurable technical advantage over B2. Provisional kill rule: on 12 founder-sealed Github cases, C should outperform independently implemented B2 by at least 3 fully-correct cases at no more than twice B2's engineering effort, without high-risk false certification or false definite negative results. Scores, cost and false claims must be reported separately. Founder's final approval required for kill/continue.
**Critical epistemic boundary:** if the provider lacks an authoritative complete enumeration, guaranteed watermark or audit continuity, **neither B2 nor C can certify non-occurrence solely from repeating queries**. Correct answer: INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.

## D-005 — Agent Incident Recall
**Status:** CHALLENGER / research only. Trace which executed actions and effects depended on a compromised skill, tool, model, delegation or policy version, determine which live authorities should be revoked and which effects need re-verification or compensation. Compare to SBOM/provenance, security incident response, audit-log correlation and existing revocation products. No novelty claim without incumbent audit.

No claims of missed events without coverage and schedule eligibility verification. Research output first; commercial demand unknown.

## D-007 — History hygiene and safe public collaboration
**Status:** ACTIVE. Previous public research branch exposed private repository identifiers in a historical import. Later edits removed those identifiers from the current tree, but GitHub's old commit references may persist. Use the clean research branch based on main. Do not merge tainted history. Do not publish secrets, customer exports or private project identifiers. Removing old references may require GitHub Support; a force-push or squash merge alone does not erase cached views or outside clones.
